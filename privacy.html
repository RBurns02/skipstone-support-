Privacy Policy

Skipstone · last updated 19 September 2026

The short version: Skipstone does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information. Your game saves to your own device and stays there. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics.

What the app stores

Skipstone keeps one save file in your device's local storage. It holds your progress and nothing else:

  • Coins, level, experience and best distance
  • Which stones, skins, gems and lakes you have unlocked
  • Upgrade levels, daily challenge progress and season trail tiers
  • The display name you choose for leaderboards, if you enter one

This never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No account, email address or password is ever requested
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers are collected
  • No contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera access
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties, because none is gathered

Purchases

Passes, bundles, stones, skins and coin packs are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles the payment; Skipstone never sees your payment details. The app asks Apple only whether a purchase completed, so it knows what to unlock. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Children

Skipstone is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here.
